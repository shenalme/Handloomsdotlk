CHILD STUDY / PERCEPTUAL EXPERIENCE

A short study of visual engagement with handloom fashion.

The project report describes a perceptual study conducted at the Perceptual Experience Lab at Cardiff School of Art and Design. Twelve participants viewed life-scale visualisations of Sri Lankan handloom drapes while eye movements were monitored, alongside preferences for colour, design patterns and outfit.

Current web study concept

The next prototype is designed as a rapid webcam-based experiment: six same-design saree colourways, brief viewing periods, webcam gaze estimation and post-viewing preference questions. This page is an information module; the experimental application can be deployed as a separate static app when the study build is ready.
